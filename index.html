It scores what is on the dashboard, live. Every score is worked out in the app from the rows on each page, and moves the moment you change one. Claude writes the content; it does not set the numbers. Development, Communication and Metrics. Progress counts for half: Completed and on-time In progress count fully, In progress past its date counts half, Not started a little, Blocked nothing. A quarter is the share of items with an owner, and a quarter the share with a target date. Dates count too. Anything not started or blocked within 14 days of its target costs up to 8 points, more as the date nears. Anything not completed after its date costs 12. At most 30 points per area. The total. A weighted average: Development ×2, Communication ×1.5, Metrics ×1. The Timeline is a plan of milestones and is not scored. Hover a score to see what makes it up. Where it breaks. It measures how organized the work is, not whether it is the right work. A plan with every box ticked can still be aimed at the wrong thing.
