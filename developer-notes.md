Current state is meant to be an MVP. Additional features are added periodically.

# How to use Scope builder

1. Start from the landing page and paste unstructured notes. Scope Builder scans your notes, asks some clarifying questions, and makes an initial build of your dashboard.
2. From there you can create and edit tasks, assignments, and metrics ownership manually. Staying on track with due dates and maintaining ownership raises your delivery health scores.
3. You can also add more notes and context. Scope Builder will use AI to add or adjust tasks, assignments, due dates, and metrics automatically based on your input.
4. Continue to edit manually as the project progresses, and/or add additional context when it makes sense.

# Notes on scoring

- Higher scores indicate your project's tasks and metrics are assigned to owners, and are on track to be completed by the target date.
- Please keep in mind that scores do not assess your program's projected effectiveness, which of course relies on your team's execution, something Scope Builder cannot discern.
